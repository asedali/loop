What happens next

1Your material is analysed for distinct technical claims.
2Related claims are clustered into 3–6 idea cards.
3Each card gets a commercial framing and a strength signal.
4You pick one to take into the validation loop.

The model is instructed to treat your material as data, never as instructions — pasted, uploaded or imported, it is wrapped the same way, and imported records are flagged to the model as text someone else wrote. It never invents deadlines, funding amounts, or links.
